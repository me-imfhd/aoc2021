we need to transpose to

doing this we can process by partitioning by position and running count filter, case operations to get the mcb

accumulate all the mcb's to get the gamma rate by padding the chars to get gamma rate bits 
convert gamma rate bits to decimal to get gamma rate
flip the gamma rate bits and convert that to decimal to get elipson rate
multiply both to get power consumption
